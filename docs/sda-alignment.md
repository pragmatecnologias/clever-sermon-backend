Since this is MVP and feature-focused, here’s how to implement SDA alignment at the product behavior level, not architectural level.

🎯 The Goal

Across all features:

Study reports

Applications

Sermon outlines

Illustrations

Interpretive challenges

Theological themes

Cultural connections

The AI should:

Avoid Sunday-as-default worship language

Avoid immortality-of-the-soul assumptions

Avoid eternal conscious torment framing

Avoid covenant theology assumptions that conflict with SDA sanctuary theology

Avoid replacement theology framing

Avoid generic “church tradition says…” statements

Without becoming polemical.

🧠 Step 1 — Define a Doctrinal Alignment Profile

Instead of vague “be SDA,” define a structured profile.

Create an internal doctrinal context like:

SDA Doctrinal Guardrails

Sabbath is the seventh day (Saturday), not Sunday.

State of the dead = unconscious sleep.

Sanctuary doctrine is real and relevant.

Great Controversy framework may be relevant.

Prophecy (Daniel/Revelation) matters.

Avoid eternal conscious torment language.

Emphasize health, wholistic discipleship when relevant.

Law and grace harmony (not law abolished framing).

Remnant language should not be sectarian in tone.

Salvation by grace through faith in Christ alone.

This becomes your doctrinal alignment specification.

🛠 How To Apply This Across Features (Feature-Level Adjustments)
1️⃣ Study Report Adjustments

In study reports:

Instead of:

“Many Christians gather on Sunday to celebrate…”

Use neutral phrasing:

“Many Christian traditions gather on Sunday…”

Or if passage relates to Sabbath:

Surface:

Creation Sabbath context

Exodus 20 reference

Jesus and Sabbath passages

Apostolic practice references

Never automatically default to Sunday worship framing.

2️⃣ Application Engine Adjustments

Current feature: audience-based application.

Now add doctrinal alignment layer:

If application mentions worship:

Replace:

“Come to church this Sunday…”

With:

“Gather in worship this Sabbath…”

This is a simple transformation rule.

Also:

Avoid:

“Celebrate resurrection Sunday” language

“After you die you’ll immediately be in heaven” assumptions

“Eternal torment” framing

3️⃣ Interpretive Challenge Mode Adjustments

When dealing with passages like:

Luke 16 (rich man & Lazarus)

Ecclesiastes 9

1 Thessalonians 4

Daniel 8

Hebrews sanctuary passages

Instead of:

“This passage proves…”

Use:

“Different Christian traditions interpret this differently…”

Then optionally include:

Adventist interpretation summary

Other mainstream interpretations

Clearly labeled differences

This preserves intellectual integrity.

4️⃣ Theological Lens Mode (Clean Implementation)

You already plan lens mode.

Add:

Adventist (default)

Evangelical generic

Academic neutral

If Adventist lens selected:

Prioritize Sabbath references when relevant

Emphasize sanctuary typology when in Hebrews

Emphasize prophetic continuity in Daniel/Revelation

Use “Sabbath” not “Lord’s Day” as default worship term

But never attack other traditions.

5️⃣ Sermon Outline Guardrails

Add content rules:

Before finalizing outline:

Scan for:

“Sunday worship” default language

Immortal soul language

Eternal conscious torment assumptions

Replacement theology framing

If detected:

Rewrite with neutral or SDA-aligned phrasing.

🧠 How To Tell Your AI To Apply This Across the App

You don’t want to repeat instructions in every prompt.

You want a global instruction block.

📝 Prompt You Can Use

Here is a feature-focused doctrinal alignment instruction:

You are generating content for a Seventh-day Adventist Bible study and sermon preparation application.

All outputs must align with the following doctrinal guidelines unless explicitly asked to compare traditions:

The seventh-day Sabbath (Saturday) is the biblical day of worship. Do not default to Sunday worship language.

Avoid assuming the immortality of the soul. The state of the dead is described as unconscious sleep until resurrection.

Avoid eternal conscious torment language. Use biblically grounded phrasing.

Recognize the significance of the sanctuary theme in Scripture.

Treat prophetic books (Daniel and Revelation) as coherent and connected.

Avoid replacement theology framing.

Maintain salvation by grace through faith in Christ.

When discussing debated passages, clearly distinguish between interpretations and avoid asserting non-Adventist views as normative.

Maintain respectful and non-polemical tone toward other Christian traditions.

When mentioning worship gatherings, use “Sabbath” unless explicitly instructed otherwise.

If a passage is commonly interpreted differently across traditions, present interpretations clearly labeled and avoid asserting one as universally accepted.

All study insights must remain grounded in Scripture.

🎯 Important: Keep It Guardrails, Not Aggression

You do NOT want:

“Other traditions are wrong.”

You want:

“Many Christian traditions interpret this differently. In Adventist theology…”

That keeps the tool credible and mature.
